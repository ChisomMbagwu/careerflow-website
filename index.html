What do you want to study?

Young people face this question earlier than most of us think, and too often they answer it with whatever their parents said, whatever their friends picked or whatever their scores allow.

Junior secondary

At the end of JSS3, students choose a department (Science, Arts or Commercial) for senior secondary. That one choice shapes the subjects they take and the courses open to them later.

Senior secondary

From SS1 to SS3, students choose the course they'll apply for, the subjects they'll write in JAMB and WAEC, and the path they'll follow after school.

65%of students choose university courses without fully understanding them
3 in 5graduates end up working in fields unrelated to their degree
Under 10%of Nigerian students have access to a career counsellor

Three programmes, one journey

From the JSS3 department choice to a first job interview, we meet students at each decision point.

Discovery
For JSS3 to SS3 students

Career Discovery

Helping JSS3 students choose the right department for senior secondary, and helping SS1 to SS3 students understand their options and map university pathways based on their strengths and interests.

  • Career assessments
  • Department and subject choice guidance
  • University pathway mapping
  • Strength discovery workshops
Exposure
For secondary school students and school leavers

Career Exposure Series

Connecting students with professionals through industry talks, mentorship sessions and career storytelling, so they can see the full range of paths open to them.

  • Industry professional talks
  • Mentorship sessions
  • Career storytelling panels
  • Workplace visits
Readiness
For undergraduates and young graduates

Career Readiness

Preparing university students and graduates for the workplace with practical skills in CV writing, interview preparation and professional networking.

  • CV development
  • Interview preparation
  • LinkedIn optimisation
  • Workplace skills training

A nonprofit built for young people at a crossroads

Career Flow Guidance Counselling and Skills Development Initiative is a registered, volunteer-led nonprofit. We work with secondary school students, school leavers and young graduates so they can choose a path on purpose and walk into it prepared.

Career guidanceMentorshipProfessional exposurePractical skills

Our work contributes to SDG 4 (Quality Education) and SDG 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth).
